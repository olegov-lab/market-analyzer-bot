Privacy Policy for BTC Monitor Telegram Bot

Last updated: 09 May 2026

1. Introduction

BTC Monitor (“the Bot”) is a Telegram bot that provides analytical information about Bitcoin, including indicators, on-chain metrics, news, and educational materials. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Bot collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

2. Data We Collect

The Bot does not collect, store, or process any personal data of users outside Telegram.

The Bot may receive the following technical data automatically provided by Telegram:

  • User ID
  • Username (if available)
  • Messages sent to the Bot

This data is used only to process commands and provide responses.

3. How We Use Data

The Bot uses received data exclusively to:

  • respond to user commands;
  • generate analytical information;
  • improve the quality and relevance of responses.

No data is transferred to third parties.

4. Data Storage

The Bot does not store user messages or personal information on external servers. All processing occurs in real time and is not saved in a persistent database.

5. Third-Party Services

The Bot may use public APIs and external data sources for:

  • market and price data;
  • on-chain metrics;
  • news feeds and educational content.

These services do not receive any personal data from users of the Bot.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has interacted with the Bot, you may contact the developer to discuss any concerns.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure that no user data is stored or shared. Since the Bot does not maintain persistent databases of user information, the risks related to data breaches are minimized.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page. Continued use of the Bot after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Bot, you can contact the developer via Telegram: @Market04ekBot.
